## The hook

Stops the thumb for all four, unchanged from earlier reads. It has a number
they can feel (£590m vs £155m), a "you" ("You hear 'cap' and picture..."),
and a clean twist (one line for everybody vs. 85% of your own). Nothing
missing.

## What would fix it (direction only, never the sentence)

1. **Section 3 (`jargon-buster`)'s title says "Four terms, in plain English"
   and the card defines three** (Football revenue, Squad Cost Ratio, PSR).
   Two of three core readers count on the first pass and both catch the
   mismatch — it is the section's own headline text, not a buried detail.
   Either retitle it to match the three terms shown, or restore a fourth.
2. **The fourth term is the one the issue can least afford to skip.**
   "Amortisation" appears bare in the hero's caption and axis label
   (`"wages plus player amortisation"`, `"Squad cost as a share of revenue
   (wages plus player amortisation)"`) with no dedicated gloss anywhere in
   the draft — only a periphrasis one section earlier ("the transfer fees,
   spread over the contracts") that never uses the word itself. Add
   "Amortisation" as the jargon-buster's fourth term, which both fixes the
   count and glosses the one term of art the hero relies on and never names
   in plain terms.
3. **Section 6's `plain` line still bundles two ideas in one sentence** —
   what the axis plots, and that the official figure would in fact sit
   *lower* than what's drawn. Moving it out of the caption (done since an
   earlier pass) fixed the caption; the sentence itself still costs Meera
   and Sana a beat. Split it: one sentence for what the chart plots, a
   second, short one for the direction of the caveat.
