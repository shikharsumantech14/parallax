## The hook

Stopped the thumb for all four. It has a number they can feel (£590m vs
£155m), a "you" ("You hear 'cap' and picture..."), and a clean twist (one
line for everybody vs. 85% of your own). Sana called it out specifically as
the kind of line that would make her keep scrolling instead of stopping — no
complaints from any reader here. Nothing missing.

## What would fix it (direction only, never the sentence)

1. **Section 6 (the hero) carries Q2's hardest fact and never prints it.**
   Manchester City's and Bournemouth's actual 2024/25 revenue (694/182) only
   exist as unlabelled dot positions on the scatter plot — no caption, intro
   or annotation states either figure in words. Put both raw revenue numbers
   into prose somewhere upstream (section 2's caption is the natural home,
   since it already does the 85% arithmetic in the other direction).
2. **Section 3's Squad Cost Ratio definition is one formula sentence with no
   worked example**, unlike every other glossary term in the card, which is
   why two readers could name the term but not restate what it means. Give it
   the same "two families" concreteness the section's own intro promises —
   one number run through the formula, the way row 2 already does for the 85%
   share.
3. **The "profit on player sales" clause that completes the 85% rule sits
   only in the hero's caption (section 6), several sections after the rule is
   first stated (section 2).** Two of four readers never carried it forward.
   State it once, plainly, where the rule is first introduced, and let the
   hero's caption repeat it rather than introduce it.
