# Sports — source allowlist

Trusted sources for football (primary), cricket, and the politics/economics
of sport. Bias toward analytical and structural reporting.

## Football — primary

- **The Athletic** — https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/ — type: digital (paywall) — focus: tactical, financial, deep
- **Tifo Football / Tifo IRL** — https://tifofootball.com/ — type: digital — focus: tactics, structures
- **The Coaches' Voice** — https://www.coachesvoice.com/cv/ — type: digital — focus: tactical analysis from practitioners
- **Statsbomb** — https://statsbomb.com/articles/ — type: industry — focus: data, xG, structural analysis
- **The Guardian — Football** — https://www.theguardian.com/football — type: newspaper — focus: news + opinion

## Football — economics / governance

- **The Swiss Ramble** — https://swissramble.substack.com/ — type: newsletter — focus: club finances
- **Forbes — SportsMoney** — https://www.forbes.com/sportsmoney/ — type: magazine — focus: business of sport
- **Off The Pitch** — https://offthepitch.com/ — type: digital (paywall) — focus: football governance + business

## Cricket — primary

- **ESPN Cricinfo** — https://www.espncricinfo.com/ — type: digital — focus: news, stats, analysis
- **The Cricket Monthly** — https://www.thecricketmonthly.com/ — type: magazine — focus: longform cricket
- **The Cricketer** — https://www.thecricketer.com/ — type: magazine — focus: cricket reporting

## India sport specialist

- **Sportstar** — https://sportstar.thehindu.com/ — type: magazine — focus: Indian sport
- **Scroll.in — Field** — https://scroll.in/field — type: digital — focus: Indian sport analysis
