# Space — source allowlist

Trusted sources for space, orbital mechanics, missions, debris, launches.

## Primary news sources

- **SpaceNews** — https://spacenews.com/ — type: digital — focus: industry news, launches, policy
- **Ars Technica — Science (Space)** — https://arstechnica.com/science/ — type: digital — focus: deep technical
- **Space.com** — https://www.space.com/ — type: digital — focus: news roundup
- **Astronomy Magazine** — https://www.astronomy.com/ — type: magazine — focus: astrophysics
- **Sky & Telescope** — https://skyandtelescope.org/ — type: magazine — focus: observational astronomy

## Primary data / official

- **NASA Newsroom** — https://www.nasa.gov/news/ — type: official — focus: missions, science releases
- **ESA News** — https://www.esa.int/Newsroom — type: official — focus: European missions, space environment reports
- **ISRO** — https://www.isro.gov.in/ — type: official — focus: Indian space program
- **SpaceX Updates** — https://www.spacex.com/updates/ — type: official — focus: Starlink, Starship
- **Jonathan McDowell's Space Report** — https://planet4589.org/ — type: blog (expert) — focus: launch data, debris

- **Secure World Foundation** — https://swfound.org/ — type: think-tank — focus: space policy, sustainability
