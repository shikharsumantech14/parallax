# Travel — source allowlist

Trusted sources for travel longform — places, crossings, rituals, infrastructure.
Bias toward sourced narrative reporting; avoid sponsored content / influencer roundups.

## Primary longform / narrative

- **Condé Nast Traveler** — https://www.cntraveler.com/ — type: magazine — focus: longform travel
- **Atlas Obscura** — https://www.atlasobscura.com/ — type: digital — focus: unusual places
- **AFAR** — https://www.afar.com/ — type: magazine — focus: cultural travel
- **Roads & Kingdoms** — https://roadsandkingdoms.com/ — type: digital — focus: food + travel reporting
- **The New York Times — Travel** — https://www.nytimes.com/section/travel — type: newspaper — focus: feature reporting

## India + South Asia specialist

- **Outlook Traveller** — https://www.outlooktraveller.com/ — type: magazine — focus: India + neighbors
- **Live History India** — https://www.livehistoryindia.com/ — type: digital — focus: heritage, history-of-place
- **Mint Lounge — Travel** — https://lifestyle.livemint.com/travel — type: newspaper supplement — focus: thoughtful India travel

## Industry / data

- **Skift** — https://skift.com/ — type: industry — focus: travel industry, infrastructure
- **CN Traveler — News** — https://www.cntraveler.com/news — type: digital — focus: travel industry news
- **The Points Guy** — https://thepointsguy.com/ — type: digital — focus: airline / hotel industry

## Geographic / cultural

- **National Geographic — Travel** — https://www.nationalgeographic.com/travel/ — type: magazine — focus: deep cultural
- **Lonely Planet — Articles** — https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles — type: digital — focus: destination reporting
