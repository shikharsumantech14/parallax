# Earth — source allowlist

Trusted sources for climate, oceans, biodiversity, geology, atmosphere.

## Primary news sources

- **Carbon Brief** — https://www.carbonbrief.org/ — type: digital — focus: climate science, deeply sourced
- **Inside Climate News** — https://insideclimatenews.org/ — type: digital — focus: investigative climate
- **Yale Climate Connections** — https://yaleclimateconnections.org/ — type: academic-digital — focus: explainers
- **Mongabay** — https://news.mongabay.com/ — type: digital — focus: tropical biodiversity, conservation
- **Grist** — https://grist.org/ — type: digital — focus: climate + justice
- **The Guardian — Environment** — https://www.theguardian.com/environment — type: newspaper — focus: global environment

## Primary scientific / official

- **NASA Earth Observatory** — https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/ — type: official — focus: satellite data, climate
- **NOAA Newsroom** — https://www.noaa.gov/news — type: official — focus: weather, oceans, atmosphere
- **IPCC Reports** — https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/ — type: scientific — focus: climate consensus
- **WMO News** — https://wmo.int/news — type: official — focus: weather, climate
- **Copernicus Climate Change Service** — https://climate.copernicus.eu/ — type: official — focus: European climate data

## India-specific

- **Centre for Science and Environment (CSE)** — https://www.cseindia.org/ — type: think-tank — focus: India environment
- **India Spend — Environment** — https://www.indiaspend.com/ — type: digital — focus: data-driven environmental
- **Down To Earth** — https://www.downtoearth.org.in/ — type: magazine — focus: India environment, longform
