# Tech — source allowlist

Trusted sources for AI/ML, infrastructure, platforms, protocols, shipping
products. Bias toward primary technical reporting and official changelogs
over hot-take commentary.

## Primary news sources

- **Ars Technica — Tech** — https://arstechnica.com/ — type: digital — focus: deep technical
- **The Verge — Tech** — https://www.theverge.com/tech — type: digital — focus: product + culture
- **TechCrunch** — https://techcrunch.com/ — type: digital — focus: startups, funding
- **The Information** — https://www.theinformation.com/ — type: digital (paywall) — focus: tech industry inside reporting
- **Stratechery** — https://stratechery.com/ — type: blog — focus: strategy analysis
- **Platformer** — https://www.platformer.news/ — type: newsletter — focus: tech + policy

## AI / ML specific

- **Anthropic Newsroom** — https://www.anthropic.com/news — type: official — focus: model releases, research
- **OpenAI Blog** — https://openai.com/blog — type: official — focus: model releases
- **Google DeepMind Blog** — https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/ — type: official — focus: research
- **Hugging Face Blog** — https://huggingface.co/blog — type: industry — focus: open models
- **Simon Willison's Blog** — https://simonwillison.net/ — type: blog — focus: practical LLM analysis
