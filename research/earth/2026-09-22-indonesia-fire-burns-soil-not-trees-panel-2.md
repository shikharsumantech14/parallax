## What would fix it (direction only, never the sentence)

1. **Section 7 (power-flow), caption:** "Three of the four bands convert carbon dioxide into carbon, multiplying by 12 over 44. India's band is its own fossil emissions on that arithmetic" still makes three of four readers do a small reconciliation — which band is the exception, and what "that arithmetic" refers to. The intro already tells the reader the punchline (India's band is widest, Indonesia's is a fraction); the caption's arithmetic aside could drop the "three of the four" framing entirely and just state what India's band represents, leaving the conversion mechanics to the source line.
2. **Section 1 (benchmark-chart), caption:** the season-so-far comparison (76 vs 85 vs 333 vs 800) and the India comparison (8.7%) are both good on their own, but landing together in the very first graphic of the issue makes every reader pause once, even if nobody fails the quiz over it. Consider whether the India 8.7% sentence is pulling weight here that section 7 (which already carries the fuller India comparison) could carry alone, freeing this caption to do one job.
3. **The hook:** unchanged since the first pass, and Sana's "barely" is unchanged with it. It has the number and the twist the contract asks for, but no image she can picture before she has read a word of the issue — the fix is not more information, it's a concrete thing to see (the "school ruler" or "tap left running" register, one section early).
