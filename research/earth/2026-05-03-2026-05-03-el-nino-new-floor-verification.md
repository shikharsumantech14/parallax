## Overall verdict

**BLOCKED.** Two ❌ flags prevent publication. First: the quote section's followup text places a Celeste Saulo quote "the same week" as the April 24 WMO El Niño announcement, but the dossier (§5 and §6) unambiguously dates that quote to the March 23, 2026 State of Global Climate 2025 press release — roughly five weeks earlier. This mislocates a real quote in time, implying Saulo was commenting on the El Niño forecast when she was actually speaking at the SOGC launch. Second: the "WHAT COMES NEXT" prose section uses a ~1.7°C potential 2027 anomaly figure that the researcher explicitly flagged as [UNVERIFIED] (dossier §9, researcher note 6); the draft includes an inline caveat but no `# EDITOR:` MDX comment, as required for unverified items. Beyond these two blockers, three ⚠️ IMPRECISE items and two mild voice flags need attention before the issue is ready to publish.

## Required fixes before publish

1. **[❌ BLOCKER] Fix the temporal placement of the Celeste Saulo quote.** In the quote section followup, change "The same week, WMO Secretary-General Celeste Saulo…" to something that accurately locates the quote in March 2026. The dossier (§5, §6) places this quote at the March 23 WMO State of Global Climate 2025 press release — approximately five weeks before the April 24 El Niño announcement. Suggested fix: *"When the WMO released its annual climate report in March, Secretary-General Celeste Saulo placed the structural framing on record: 'Human activities are increasingly disrupting…'"* or equivalent accurate temporal anchor.

2. **[❌ BLOCKER] Add a `# EDITOR:` MDX comment for the 1.7°C 2027 figure.** The researcher marked this [UNVERIFIED] (dossier §9, note 6). The draft prose already caveats it inline, which is good — but add a formal `{/* # EDITOR: 1.7°C figure is unverified — dossier §9 note 6. Trace to primary source or remove before publish. */}` comment immediately preceding the sentence. Alternatively, remove the number entirely and keep only the structural argument ("the probability for 2027 is higher") which is fully supported.

3. **[⚠️] Revise the Nov 2015 timeline note.** "First major ENSO event of the satellite era to push global temperatures into a new regime" is not in the dossier and is potentially inaccurate — the satellite era (from ~1979) includes the strong 1982–83 and 1997–98 El Niños. The dossier simply says "Strongest El Niño in 18 years." Align the note with the dossier characterization, e.g.: *"Strongest El Niño in 18 years. 2016 became the warmest year on record at ~1.25°C above pre-industrial."*

4. **[⚠️] Specify the Arctic sea ice partner in the joint record.** The data-readout tile note says "Joint-smallest record in nearly 50 years of satellite data" without naming the year it is tied with. The dossier specifies "joint-smallest record *with 2025*." Add "with 2025" to the tile note so readers understand both years are tied.

5. **[⚠️] Restore the approximate qualifier on the 30% probability.** The dossier source reports "~30%." The data tile shows a precise "30." Either add a tilde in the note text or reword the note: "Approximately 30% chance — the line first crossed in 2024."
