# Jargon — terms of art that must be explained on first use

> **DRAFT · 2026-09-13.** Created under `docs/REGISTER-PLAN.md` §7.1 (the
> JARGON-UNGLOSSED check) and `_voice-core.md` §3 rule 2: every term of art is
> explained the moment it appears, in the same sentence or the next. This list
> seeds the gate; the writers keep it open. A term appearing before its gloss
> is a flag. Add a row whenever a new term enters an issue. The gloss column
> is a *suggested* plain phrase — the writer may do better, in the register.
> Removing this DRAFT line is the signature.

## Format

`term | where it turns up | a plain gloss (≤ 20 words) | Indian comparison if one helps`

The gate matches the term case-insensitively, whole word, and looks for a
gloss marker within the same or the next sentence: a dash, a colon, *matlab*,
*that means*, *which is*, *in other words*, or the term in brackets after its
plain description.

## How to add a row

Whenever a new term enters a dossier, the researcher adds it here with the
plainest true gloss the sources support. The drafter uses the gloss or a
better one. The verifier checks the gloss does not misstate the mechanism —
an analogy is a claim.
