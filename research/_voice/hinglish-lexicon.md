## How spellings were chosen

The spelling most people type on a phone, not the most "correct"
transliteration: *kyunki* not *kyonki*, *zyada* not *jyada*, *samajh* not
*samaj* (which is a different word: society). When two spellings are common,
the shorter one. The lockfile method in the plan (a one-time, offline,
human-reviewed transliteration round-trip) can extend this list later; until
then this is the list.

## Banned on the reading page

| Word | Why |
|---|---|
| yaar, bhai, bro, dude | register; at most once per issue on sports or travel, never politics or earth |
| dosto, namaskar | the YouTube intro |
| samjhe?, simple hai na?, right? | condescension |
| shayad, lagbhag | they hedge; a sourced claim is never hedged in Hindi |
| jhoot, chor, ghotala | accusation words; the verifier blocks advocacy |
| any word in Devanagari | the typeface has none |
| any Hindi word in a caption, how-to-read, plain line, source line or data label | the precision layer is English only |

## Units and numbers (English, always)

₹, lakh, crore, km, kg, °C. Never a Hindi numeral word (*das, sau, hazaar*)
beside a figure. Dollar figures carry the rupee equivalent in the same
sentence.
