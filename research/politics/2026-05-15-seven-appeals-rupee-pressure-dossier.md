## 1. Structural argument (the Parallax take)

The reader arrives thinking this is a story about a prime minister making a patriotic appeal in a moment of crisis — a political performance for the cameras. The structural truth is older and harder: India has made this exact request before, in 1962, in 1971, and most dramatically in 1991, when the RBI secretly airlifted 46.91 tonnes of gold to the Bank of England to avoid sovereign default. The mechanism that keeps producing these moments is not a failure of individual citizen restraint; it is a balance-of-payments architecture that was partially redesigned after 1991 — India liberalised, built reserves, and integrated into global capital markets — but never structurally reduced its dependence on three commodity imports (crude oil, gold, edible oil) that together now account for roughly half of India's merchandise import bill. When an external shock arrives, as it did on February 28, 2026 when US-Israel strikes on Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz and drove Brent crude from $72 to $120 per barrel, the structural exposure surfaces immediately: forex reserves fall, the rupee slides to record lows, and the only lever the government reaches for that doesn't require legislative action is an appeal to the citizen. The Parallax take is that Modi's seven appeals are not a one-off; they are the symptomatic output of a recurring structural deficit between India's consumption profile and its production base that 35 years of post-1991 reforms have not resolved.

## 2. Why now

- **May 10, 2026:** PM Modi, at the BJP public meeting at Secunderabad Parade Grounds, Hyderabad, makes seven appeals to citizens — urging them not to buy gold for one year, avoid foreign travel, use public transport, work from home, reduce cooking oil and fertiliser consumption, and buy swadeshi over imported branded goods. Framing: "Patriotism is not only about the willingness to sacrifice one's life on the border. In these times, it is about living responsibly and fulfilling our duties to the nation."
- **May 13, 2026:** Three days later, the government doubles gold and silver import duty from 6% to 15% (basic customs duty 5% → 10%; AIDC 1% → 5%; effective total duty including 3% IGST rises from 9.18% to 18.45%), a concrete fiscal follow-through to the verbal appeal.
- **Backdrop:** Brent crude at ~$114/barrel by April 30, 2026 (peak intraday ~$120+); rupee at record low of 95.74–95.96 per dollar on May 13; forex reserves down ~$37.8 billion from $728.49 billion (Feb 27) to $690.69 billion (May 1); CAD projected to widen to 2.2% of GDP in FY27 (Crisil) from 0.8% in FY26.

## 4. Key facts & data

**The seven appeals (verbatim list from Modi, May 10, 2026):**
1. Prioritise work from home
2. Avoid buying gold for one year
3. Use metro and public transport (reduce petrol/diesel consumption)
4. Cut cooking oil consumption / reduce fertiliser use
5. Reduce chemical fertiliser consumption
6. Buy swadeshi over foreign-branded goods
7. Avoid foreign travel for one year
— [The Print](https://theprint.in/india/pm-advocates-economic-self-defence-use-fuel-sparingly-avoid-buying-gold-postpone-foreign-travel/2927438/); [Organiser.org via web search](https://organiser.org/2026/05/11/352842/bharat/pm-modis-7-appeals-stress-work-from-home-swadeshi-fuel-savings-and-avoiding-gold-purchases-amid-global-uncertainty/)

**Gold import duty change (May 13, 2026):**
- Old rate: 6% (basic customs duty) + AIDC 1% + 3% IGST = ~9.18% effective
- New rate: 10% basic customs duty + AIDC 5% + 3% IGST = 18.45% effective
- Platinum duty: 6.4% → 15.4%
— [The Print](https://theprint.in/economy/import-duty-on-gold-silver-more-than-doubled-to-curb-imports-support-rupee/2930040/)

**India's forex reserves decline:**
- Peak: $728.49 billion (February 27, 2026 — day before war began)
- As of May 1, 2026: $690.69 billion
- Total decline: ~$37.8 billion in ~9 weeks
- RBI intervention pace: ~$5-6 billion per week in spot/forward markets
- Prior year total RBI sales: over $100 billion in spot and forward markets during FY25-26
— [Multibagg/RBI data](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/india-forex-reserves-fall-may1-cmp0t7nvfitezpn0j4357m32z); [The Print data explainer](https://theprint.in/india/fuel-gold-fertiliser-foreign-travel-data-9-charts-reveal-whats-behind-modis-appeal-cut-the-clutter/2929914/)

- Record low: 95.74–95.96 per dollar (intraday, May 13, 2026); earlier cited at 95.63 (May 12 article)
