## Required fixes before publish

1. **Correct the analyst CAD range in the data-readout tile.** The note for the 2.2% tile states "Analyst range: 1.3% to 2.4%." The dossier (§4) gives the independent analyst range as "1.5-2.4%." The 1.3% figure is the CEA's own government estimate, not an outside analyst view. Change to "Analyst range: 1.5% to 2.4%" (and retain the separate CEA attribution at 1.3% as already written).

2. **Correct the date associated with the $690.69B forex reserve figure in Timeline 1.** The Apr 30 event note attributes the reserve decline ("fallen to approximately $690.69 billion — down $37.8 billion in nine weeks") to April 30. The dossier (§4 and §3) consistently dates this figure to May 1, 2026. Move the reserve figure to a standalone May 1 event entry, or change the note to read "as of May 1, 2026."

3. **Resolve the 367-gram Indira Gandhi figure.** The dossier §9 item 2 marks this [UNVERIFIED] against allowlisted sources (the Scroll.in article returned HTTP 403; the only trace is via BusinessToday, which is outside the allowlist). The draft uses the figure with no EDITOR flag. Either: (a) add a `# EDITOR: verify 367-gram figure against primary source before publishing — currently cited only via BusinessToday, not on allowlist` flag in the timeline note, or (b) remove the specific gram figure and retain only the general claim that Indira Gandhi donated gold jewellery.

4. **Resolve the 25,000-tonne household gold figure.** The dossier §9 item 3 marks this [UNVERIFIED] because the WGC July 2023 report was not directly fetched via allowlisted sources. The draft uses it with a qualifier but no EDITOR flag. Either: (a) add a `# EDITOR: WGC 25,000-tonne figure not fetchable from allowlisted source in this research pass — verify against WGC published report before printing` flag in the prose paragraph, or (b) soften to "tens of thousands of tonnes by WGC estimates" without specifying the exact figure.

5. **Restore the seven-appeal enumeration in Timeline 1.** The dossier §4 lists cooking oil reduction and chemical fertiliser reduction as two separate appeals. The draft's May 10 timeline note merges them ("Reduce cooking oil and fertiliser consumption"), representing only six distinct items in the bullet. Separate them to maintain accuracy and consistency with the seven-appeal framing that is the piece's structural premise.

## Optional improvements

- The Timeline 2 (THREE TIMES BEFORE) label for the May 13 entry reads "Gold import duty doubled to 15% effective rate." The dossier treats 15% as the combined BCD+AIDC shorthand, while the total effective duty including IGST is 18.45%. The note in the same entry correctly states 18.45%, but calling 15% the "effective rate" in the label is technically inconsistent. Consider revising the label to "Gold import duty: combined rate to 15%; effective 18.45% with IGST" for precision — not a blocker but reduces potential reader confusion.

- The comparison section's "Not done" column could be strengthened by a sourced note that no petrol/diesel price revision has been announced (e.g., citing PIB or MoPNG), as the dossier §9 notes this would need sourcing from a press release to be fully documented.

- The dossier flags a second verbatim Nageswaran quote ("Managing the current account credibly, financing it, and preventing further currency depreciation are the central macroeconomic imperatives of FY27") that does not appear in the draft. The quote section uses only the "stress test" quote. The second quote could strengthen the quote section or the followup text, but its absence is not an error.
