## The hook

> "2 crore followers in a week. One order made the account vanish from your
> phone, and from nobody else's."

**Sana — yes, and harder than before.** This is the change I asked for and it
did what I said it would. "2 crore" as a numeral is the shape her eye catches

**"2 crore" does not read as trying too hard.** Numeral-plus-Indian-unit is the
standard orthography for this quantity in Indian print and in speech — nobody
writes "two crore" in a headline. It is also correctly an Indian figure
(an Indian account's Indian following), so contract rule 4 asks for crore and
would accept crore alone. The head gives both units, which is generous rather
than required.

**Aarav, Meera, Karthik — yes.** Karthik still reads "vanish" against the
title's "hidden" and takes the softer word as the true one, which is correct.

**The dek, asked about directly: it is now a gloss, where it used to be pure
repetition.** Before the stylist, the hook and the dek opened on the identical
five words with the identical number, which I flagged as repetition. Now the
hook says "2 crore" and the dek says "Twenty million", so reading down the head
delivers the conversion free, at the moment the reader first meets the number.
That is a real gain and the change should stand.

What remains is the *frame*, not the units: the two lines share a five-word
tail ("followers in a week"), so the eye re-reads words it already has before
the dek's new clause arrives. And the dek's second clause, "The order reached
one country", is the fourth statement of the one-country finding on a screen
that already has the title ("in India, nowhere else"), the hook ("from nobody
else's") and, immediately below, §1's "1 country" and its caption ("No other
country did"). Five statements of one finding before the argument moves.

Nobody is confused by this — it is the issue's finding and repetition teaches
it. It is why the first screen reads slow for Meera, and the dek is the one
head field that is free to change: at eleven words, any eleven-word dek costs
the budget nothing and cannot touch the 80-word floor, which is set by the
