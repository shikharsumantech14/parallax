## CANON amendments (applied 2026-07-05)

1. **Type deployment rhythm (extends §5):** within one section, body-register
   text is ONE size/leading (`--prose-size` 16px/1.68). Mono appears at most
   twice per section (eyebrow + cite/source); never as flow text. Serif
   beyond the title only for quotes/annotations.
2. **Justified prose (extends §5):** body paragraphs (`.px-prose`, intros,
   followups, primer) justify with `hyphens: auto` at ≥640px; `text-wrap:
   pretty` always. Headlines/quotes/legends never justify.
3. **Component text budget (new §4.5):** a viz component's on-screen text ≤
   ~80 words at rest (caption + plain + the marks' labels). Everything else
   moves behind interaction (tooltip/expand) or below the fold (legend
   collapses past 5 rows behind a "show all" disclosure). A component that
   needs a paragraph to be understood is a failed FORM — redesign the form.
4. **Section intro budget:** showcase/demo intros ≤ 1 sentence; real-issue
   intros ≤ 3 (existing rule, now enforced by the stylist).
