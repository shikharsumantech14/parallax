# Composer memory digest
Digest of the memory as of 2026-09-28. Read-only during a run. Updates happen in a post-review pass.

## Fits and misfits
- The hero draws what the argument adds, never the title's number: a depth column, a stack, the denominator as an axis.
- Who holds which lever: power-matrix, not comparison. One case, not dated regimes. An empty row is its strongest mark.
- Two values per entity: one benchmark-chart bar per gap, both levels in the sublabel.
- A paradox needs two true facts pulling apart. A misread is a you-think, a cause and its consequence a three-steps.
- A series with unverified gaps: timeline events, never an interpolated line.
- launch-stats labels any data "launches / yr". Never break a kind's DON'T USE, even to buy a floor.

## Thin dossiers and operator rulings
- Totals cannot feed a per-minute or monthly instrument or a modelled surface. carbon-loop needs stocks and fluxes in one unit, so take power-flow or three-steps. Name the missing field and the fetch that unblocks it.
- A band is not a value. Draw the edge that weakens the claim (usually the low end) on every mark, never the midpoint, with the band in the label or caption. Operator ruling.
- Never manufacture a figure: no rate across two dates, no base from a ratio (ratios go in number-sense), no hours from a percentage (margin-bullets takes shares against targets).
- A vote with no recorded or verified split cannot feed vote-result or vote-flow.
- One reporting year per graphic. No sourced FX rate, no rupee comparison. Never an [UNVERIFIED] hero: keep it mid-spine with a one-for-one swap.
- region-map ids are ISO 3166-1 numeric strings and a wrong one vanishes, so list them. benchmark-chart prints values raw: give big counts in lakh or crore.
- A card cut by the cap, not the data: say so, and put its payload in a graphic's intro.

## Floor and budget traps
- Plan jargon-buster, three-steps and comparison as text-only: the draft check scores them so, for visual share and adjacency. Never jargon-buster beside three-steps: fold the steps into the glosses.
- timeline, bill-breakdown, bill-passage and power-matrix score as drawn graphics. data-readout is a card outside the cap of three.
- Size the spine from the visual beats: 4 allow at most six rows, 5 at most eight. Fix a miss by cutting a text row, never adding one.
- A dossier's spine can miss its key beat and miscount floors. Rebuild it.
- The 1,100 also counts every eyebrow, title and source label (about 15 words a row), plus authored plain, how-to-read and data labels.
- Words before the first graphic include row 1's eyebrow and title: head at most 69, no intro on row 1.
- Budget about 25 words of authored copy for a graphic off its home desk or axis. Defaults for throughput-dial, benchmark-chart, scaling-plot and power-flow often mislead.
- Names in chart labels and sublabels escape the name count, but readers still meet them.
- Name one slack row, usually the quote.
