# Stylist memory digest
Digest of the memory as of 2026-09-28. Read-only during a run. Updates happen in a post-review pass.

## Jobs
- Count the CONVERSATIONAL half. Storyboards miscount, and nine sections need five.
- A first-slot graphic with no intro is INVESTIGATION. If CONVERSATIONAL is short, move INVESTIGATION to a hero whose point is a visible gap.
- The intro's first move decides the job. One that restates the last graphic or opens on an analogy is CONVERSATIONAL.
- `paradox` and a closing `prose` are CALM-STRUCTURAL. `number-sense` against a far larger scale is AWE, except on politics.

## Recurring tells
- "X, not Y" is the most overspent ration. Keep the storyboard's one, state the rest plainly. "X rather than Y" counts, and a `you-think` card is already one.
- The commonest find is an implied because, but or so. Write it.
- A panel fix welded on with a colon leaves one long sentence or three numerals in a clause. Split it.
- Three intros opening alike ("Here is", "So") are a tic.
- On any other sport, replace cricket words (delivery, over, pitch, strike).

## Hindi and desks
- Zero Hindi is correct. Never add a word for a count or flag a head for having none.
- Cutting the English beside a Hindi word makes it load-bearing. Keep both or cut the Hindi.
- An abstract noun may follow its English noun as "that kharcha" in the next clause. The one-clause form failed a panel.
- The operator kept `aasmaan` beside "up there". As the only sky-word it failed and became "sky".
- An authorised word is only permission. `matlab` was cut where a card title already said "so".
- Politics takes at most one phrase, as reported speech glossed in the sentence. Flag Hindi in a title, which the contents list prints bare.

## Boundaries
- Return every non-prose field byte-identical, or the script refuses the output.
- A fix anywhere else (note, label, caption, annotation, title, head) is a flag with exact old and new wording, whoever asked. Name any `at` or cell matching a flagged label.
- A term locked in a title, eyebrow, label, note or quote is glossed in prose the reader meets before it. A quoted acronym is expanded in the framing sentence.
- A caption fix can often go in its section's intro. If that repeats the caption, flag the old copy for deletion.
- A fact lands only in a section whose `sourceRefs` back it. At an unsourced closer, restate an earlier drawn point.
- The intro carries what the numbers need to be read right, such as their base, or that two units of one quantity never convert. A drawing choice goes in `plain`, without numerals. Above the graphic it reads as a hedge.
- Panel seam fixes usually arrive unapplied and are yours. A fix naming a figure the draft lacks, or its unsourced half, is a flag.
- On harm done to the issue's people, name the victim as an object and give the penalty its unit.
- Keep each numeral's count, a passing year included. Fund additions from duplicates, never from glosses.
