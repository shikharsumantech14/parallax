          The UN's statistics manual said why in 2008. Nationals living abroad,
          it wrote, "mainly stay with family and friends" instead of visiting
          tourist sites. A foreign tourist stays eighteen days and pays for
          somewhere to sleep, but a cousin's spare room bills nobody.
        - >-
          Against that, India budgeted ₹3.5 crore to promote itself overseas
          this financial year. Divide it by 9.15 million foreign tourists, which
          means about ₹3.83 each.
