Overlays that sign the frame, whatever is in it.

These are empty layers, not layouts. Any video, any photograph, any infographic goes underneath; the overlay never changes. Everything is positioned against real platform chrome, so nothing lands under a caption, an action rail or a scrub bar.

Every frame is the true export size shown at reduced scale, with exact coordinates beside it so an editor can rebuild it in any tool. Drop an image on any slot to try your own content under the layer.

— 05 · which mark, where

Every placement, in delivered pixels.

The cut is decided by delivered size — how many pixels the mark occupies on the viewer's screen, not in the export. Frame fraction is the wrong variable: a horizontal frame is watched at ~1280 and a vertical one at ~400, so the same fraction delivers three times the size.

delivered = mark px × (delivery width ÷ export frame width) · at 32px or more the outline cut, below it the reversed cut.

— 06 · presence

Four tiers of transparency.

Opacity is set by what the mark is sitting on, not by taste. A mark on brand ground can stay near-solid; a mark laid directly over someone's footage has to give the frame back. The Presence control at the top scales all four tiers by ±20% at once, so a single video can be dialled without breaking their relationship.

— 07 · the rules

How to keep it subtle.

BudgetBranding never exceeds 4% of frame area outside the title card and the outro. The bug is 0.6% — 190 × 64 in a 1920 × 1080 frame. One cornerThe mark takes one corner and one rule per frame. Two brand elements in two corners reads as a template, not a publication. OpacitySet by the tier table above, never by eye. Fade the whole element together — slab and mark as one group. A solid mark on a faded slab reads as a sticker; a faded mark on a solid slab reads as a print error. MotionOpacity only, 300ms, once. No slides, no wipes, no logo animation. The mark does not spin, the crescent does not orbit.
ColourOne accent per video. Use the topic accent when the video belongs to a desk, oxide red when it does not. Never two. TypeLiterata 700 for titles, 600 tracked caps for meta. Minimum 34px on vertical, 19px on horizontal, at export size. LegibilityOver footage, always a scrim gradient behind type — never a drop shadow, never an outline stroke. An ambient-tier mark must have a scrim: at 56% it disappears on a bright frame. NeverNo logo at the head of a video — a cold open holds the viewer, a logo does not. No bug on the outro. No mark over a face.
