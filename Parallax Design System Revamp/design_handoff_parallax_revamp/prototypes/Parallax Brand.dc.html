Brand system · v2 · August 2026

One mark, six phases, two cuts. Everything in this book derives from a single dial position per desk — nothing here is drawn twice, and nothing is improvised.

— 01 · the idea

You cannot know how far away something is until you have moved.

A star's distance is measured by looking at it twice, six months apart, from opposite ends of Earth's orbit. Depth is what patience and displacement buy you — which is why this publication runs structural issues instead of reactions.

The mark is that in one shape: a disc lit from one side, with the crescent showing the part that was hidden until something moved. The letter sits in the calm middle so a stranger can read it without being told any of the above.

— 02 · construction

Four circles and a letter.

300-unit box · centre 150,150

Ringr = 118, stroke 7, ink. The outer boundary of the mark; the artboard is centred on it. Lightr = 110, accent, concentric with the ring. Occluderr = 113 — three units larger than the light, which is what makes the crescent swell at the belly and taper to points. Ground-coloured. DialThe occluder sits 15 units from centre, pushed away from the desk's angle θ, so the crescent appears at θ. This is the only number that changes between desks. LetterLiterata 700, 160 units, anchored middle at x = 150, baseline y = 207. Cap centre lands on 150 — optically centred, not box-centred. Clear space15 units — one dial radius — outside the ring on all four sides. AlignmentThe mark is symmetric about its own centre at every size. Any circular crop of radius ≥ 125 contains it with even margin.

— 03 · the dial

Six desks, six hours, 60° apart.

Politics anchors the dial at 325° and the rest follow at even intervals. Fixed once; never improvised, never reassigned.

— 04 · the cover cut

Two crescents, for print and covers only.

The same dial produces a second crescent outside the ring, on the opposite flank. It carries a shadow and a phase at once. Two accent elements is one more than the system normally permits, so this cut is reserved: covers, posters, the colophon, printed matter. Never a favicon, never an avatar, never in a masthead.

— 05 · scale and cuts

Where the mark simplifies.

96px · outline 40px · outline 24px · outline 18px · reversed 16px · reversed

The ring's stroke thickens as the mark shrinks — 7 → 10 → 14 units — so it holds its weight. Below 24px the outline and the crescent both fail, so the mark switches to the reversed cut: a solid ink disc with the letter knocked out. That is a declared tier, not a degradation.
